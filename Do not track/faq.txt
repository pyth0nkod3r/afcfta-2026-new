Individuals, startups, SMEs, and innovators across Africa, aged 18+, including hybrid (physical and virtual) participation. How do I register? Complete the online registration form via www.afcftahackathon.com/register and select your challenge track. Is there a participation fee? Participation is free. Teams are responsible for travel/accommodation for physical attendance unless specified by the organizers. 2️⃣ Challenge Tracks & Innovation Can I submit solutions for more than one challenge track? No. Each team must select one track and align their prototype/MVP to it. Are existing solutions allowed? Solutions must be original or adapted with significant innovation. Intellectual property rights remain with participants. 3️⃣ Hackathon Format What is the hybrid format? Teams can participate physically in Abuja, Nigeria, or virtually from any African country. Will virtual participants have mentorship and resources? Yes, mentors, workshops, and technical resources will be available online. 4️⃣ Rewards & Incentives What are the prizes? Top 10 teams share ₦60M, plus thematic awards, incubation support, mentorship, and integration into AfCFTA innovation networks. How will rewards be distributed? Under supervision of AfCFTA Secretariat and Finance Committee, with a transparent evaluation process. 5️⃣ Technical & Support What support is provided? Reliable internet, cloud credits, API access, and technical support during hackathon days. Who can I contact for issues? Contact the Hackathon Support Team at support@afcftahackathon.com.
