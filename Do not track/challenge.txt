CTA Buttons: 🔗 Submit Your Idea | 📘 Download Track Guide 🧭 1️⃣ Introduction: Shaping the Future of African Trade through Innovation Content: Each challenge track focuses on a critical pillar of Africa’s digital trade ecosystem. From cross-border payments to smart logistics and AI-driven compliance, participants will design, prototype, and showcase scalable innovations that bridge policy and technology. Teams are encouraged to align their ideas with the AfCFTA Digital Trade Protocol, the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System (PAPSS), and the Agenda 2063 vision for a unified African market. ⚙️ 2️⃣ All Challenge Tracks Below are the official tracks for AfCFTA Hackathon 2026. Each represents a frontier for innovation, trade, and collaboration across Africa. 1. Digital Trade Facilitation Simplify and digitize cross-border trade processes through smart automation, blockchain, and real-time customs coordination. Focus areas: trade document exchange, border integration, data interoperability, and single-window systems. 2. AI for Trade Intelligence Leverage Artificial Intelligence to generate insights on trade trends, policy impact, and market opportunities across Africa. Focus areas: predictive analytics, trade data visualization, policy simulation, and smart decision support. 3. FinTech & MSME Financing Bridge Africa’s financing gap by building inclusive digital financial tools for MSMEs and informal traders. Focus areas: credit scoring, alternative lending models, blockchain-based financing, and regional remittances. 4. E-Commerce & MSME Empowerment Enable small businesses to access continental markets through digital commerce and inclusive marketplaces. Focus areas: multi-currency e-commerce, logistics integration, digital payments, and merchant support tools. 5. Smart Logistics & Supply Chains Design logistics innovations that improve the movement of goods and reduce trade bottlenecks across borders. Focus areas: smart routing, IoT-enabled tracking, digital warehousing, and cross-border coordination. 6. Documentation & Compliance Automation Build digital solutions that simplify trade documentation, certifications, and regulatory compliance. Focus areas: digital certificates of origin, automated customs clearance, and trade data verification. 7. Sustainable & Inclusive Trade Promote green, gender-inclusive, and equitable trade practices through technology-driven solutions. Focus areas: carbon tracking, circular economy solutions, women-led trade platforms, and green supply chains. 8. Digital Identity & Verification Strengthen trust and traceability in trade through interoperable digital identity frameworks. Focus areas: cross-border KYC systems, digital credentials, and identity-linked payment authorization. 9. Agriculture & Trade Value Chain Boost agribusiness trade by digitizing agricultural value chains and improving market access for smallholder farmers. Focus areas: traceability, commodity pricing, digital cooperatives, and export facilitation. 10. Knowledge & Learning Ecosystem Foster digital trade literacy and innovation through accessible learning tools and capacity-building platforms. Focus areas: digital trade education, mentorship platforms, and AI-driven learning ecosystems. 🧩 3️⃣ Track Collaboration & Cross-Impact Participants are encouraged to form multi-disciplinary teams that combine expertise from different tracks — for example, linking FinTech + Digital Identity, or AI + Logistics — to develop holistic trade solutions that reflect real-world interconnectivity. 🏆 4️⃣ Evaluation Criteria Each solution will be evaluated based on: Innovation & Originality – Novel approach to solving a trade challenge. Relevance to AfCFTA Goals – Alignment with Digital Trade Protocol objectives. Scalability & Sustainability – Potential for real-world adoption and impact. Technical Execution – Functionality, design, and user experience. Inclusiveness – Promotion of gender, youth, and regional participation. 🚀 5️⃣ Call to Action Headline: “Your Idea Could Power Africa’s Next Digital Trade Breakthrough” Subtext: Join innovators shaping Africa’s borderless future.
